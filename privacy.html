Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how your personal data is processed when you use the Nine Lives mobile game (the "Game"). It is written to meet the requirements of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and Google Play policies.

In short: We process the minimum needed to run the Game. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We never ask for your real name, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Anonymous statistics are collected only if you agree. You can delete your account from inside the Game at any time.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Yunus Yeşilördek, the independent developer who develops and publishes the Game under the name Aku Forge ("we"). Aku Forge is not a company but the developer's publishing name. For data protection requests and any questions: forgeaku@gmail.com

4. How data is collected

Data is collected electronically from what you enter in the Game and automatically while the Game runs, through the software components of the service providers listed below.

5. Sharing and international transfers

The Game's online features run on Unity Technologies' infrastructure (Unity Gaming Services), which processes data on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Unity Authentication and Unity Player Accounts: accounts and sign-in
  • Unity Cloud Save: storing coins, looks and level
  • Unity Lobby and Relay: matchmaking and connections between players
  • Unity Remote Config: game settings and version checks
  • Unity Analytics: anonymous statistics, only with your consent

If you sign in with Google, Google also takes part in authentication under its own privacy policy.

Unity's servers may be located outside your country (including the European Union and the United States), so your data may be transferred internationally. Such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, including Unity's data processing terms and standard contractual clauses, as required by GDPR and KVKK Art. 9. More: Unity Privacy Policy.

Otherwise, data is disclosed only to authorised public authorities where legally required. We do not sell or rent your data or share it for advertising.

6. What other players see

Players in the same match see your player name, character look, in-game actions and the quick-chat lines you choose. There is no free-text or voice chat. Offensive player names are blocked automatically. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your player name.

7. Retention

  • Account and progress: until you delete your account. We may delete accounts that have not been used for 24 months.
  • Connection and match data: only for the match and connection; technical logs kept by Unity for security and operation follow Unity's retention periods.
  • Anonymous statistics: as long as needed for the purpose and within Unity Analytics' retention periods. When you withdraw consent, a deletion request is sent to Unity.
  • On-device data: removed when you uninstall the Game.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored securely by Unity and cannot be seen by us. Access is limited to what the Game needs. If we learn of a data breach, we will make the notifications required by law.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 must not use it. If we learn that we hold data of a child under 13, we will delete it. If you are under 18, you must use the Game with the permission of a parent or guardian. Where the age of digital consent in your country is higher (e.g. 16 in some EU countries), consent to anonymous statistics must be given by your parent or guardian.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces adverse effects. Under KVKK Art. 11 you may also learn whether your data is processed, for what purpose, and to whom it is transferred, and claim compensation for unlawful processing.

Send requests to forgeaku@gmail.com with enough detail to identify your account (your username or the player ID shown on the Account screen). We reply free of charge within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: Personal Data Protection Board; in the EU: your national supervisory authority).

You can switch anonymous statistics off at any time in the Game: Profile → Account → Statistics sharing.

11. Account and data deletion

If you signed in with a username or with Google, you can permanently delete your account and all data linked to it (coins, looks, level) from inside the Game: Profile → Account → Delete account. If you play as a guest, you can request deletion by e-mail. If you cannot access the Game, follow the steps on the Account & Data Deletion page to request deletion by e-mail.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game or the law changes. The current version is always published on this page. For significant changes we will inform you in the Game and ask for your consent again where required.
