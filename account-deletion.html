Nine Lives – Account & Data Deletion

Developer: Yunus Yeşilördek (Aku Forge)

In the Game (recommended)

  1. Open Nine Lives.
  2. Tap your name card at the top left to open Profile (Profil).
  3. Tap Account (Hesap) at the top right.
  4. Tap Delete account (Hesabı sil) and confirm with Yes, delete (Evet, sil).

Deletion happens immediately and needs an internet connection. The Delete account button is shown for accounts signed in with a username or with Google. If you play as a guest, use the e-mail route below.

By e-mail

If you cannot access the Game, e-mail forgeaku@gmail.com with the subject "Nine Lives account deletion" and include one of the following so we can find your account:

  • your username (if you registered with one);
  • your Google account e-mail if you signed in with Google (sending from that address is enough);
  • your player ID shown on the Account screen if you play as a guest.

Once your identity is verified, we complete the request within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

  • your game account and sign-in details (username and password, or the Google link);
  • your coins, owned looks, experience points and level;
  • your player name and on-device stats (when deleted in the Game);
  • if statistics sharing was on, a request to delete the collected statistics is sent to Unity.

What may be kept

Where we have a legal obligation (e.g. a request from an authority), the relevant records may be kept only as long as required. Technical logs kept by our provider Unity for security and operation are deleted at the end of Unity's retention periods. Deleted accounts and virtual items cannot be restored.
